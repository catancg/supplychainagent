# When to Accept a Long Lead Time

A long lead time (30+ days, typically an imported supplier) is acceptable
only when **both** of the following hold:

1. The SKU is not currently in a demand spike and current inventory plus any
   in-flight orders will cover demand through the lead time window, or the
   order is explicitly meant to rebuild baseline stock rather than cover an
   immediate shortfall.
2. The cost savings versus the next-best faster alternative are material
   (roughly 10% or more of landed cost) — a long lead time is not worth
   accepting to save a trivial amount.

During a demand spike or when a warehouse is already below its reorder
point with no other order covering the gap, prefer a faster supplier even at
higher unit cost. Speed of replenishment matters more than unit cost when a
stockout is imminent.
