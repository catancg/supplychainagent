# Stocking Policy — Major Appliances

Category: air conditioners, refrigerators, washing machines, microwaves, TVs
(SKUs AC-12, RF-21, WM-07, MW-14, TV-33 and similar high-value, slower-turnover
items).

Target safety buffer: **5 days** of average daily demand, held in addition to
expected replenishment lead time. These items are expensive to overstock, but
a stockout during a demand spike (e.g. a heat wave for air conditioners) is
highly visible and costly in lost sales, so the buffer should not be cut below
5 days even under budget pressure.

When recent demand is flagged as a spike (recent average at least 1.5x the
historical average), temporarily widen the buffer to **8 days** for the
affected SKU only, and prioritize the warehouse(s) with the lowest on-hand
inventory first.
