# Stocking Policy — Small / Fast-Turnover Items

Category: fans, heaters, blenders, vacuum cleaners, chargers (SKUs FN-05,
HT-09, BL-02, VC-18, SP-25 and similar low-cost, high-turnover items).

Target safety buffer: **3 days** of average daily demand. These items are
cheap to hold and cheap to reorder frequently, so there is no need for the
wider buffers used for major appliances. Do not over-order small items just
because budget is available — budget should be reserved for SKUs that are
actually below their reorder point.
